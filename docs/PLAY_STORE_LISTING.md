## English full description

IslamTimeWorld brings essential daily Islamic tools together in one focused app.

• Accurate prayer-time experience based on your location
• Qibla direction with compass and map-based guidance
• Quran reading with multilingual translations
• Verified Hadith content with source information
• Duas, dhikr and salawat
• Hijri calendar and prayer calendar
• Allah's 99 Names
• Qazo tracking
• Nearby mosque discovery
• Haramayn LIVE access
• Day, Night and Auto themes
• Multilingual interface with 13 canonical languages

IslamTimeWorld is designed to make everyday worship tools easy to reach while
preserving source information and clear language separation.

Location permission is used for location-aware prayer/Qibla/mosque functionality.
Notification permission is used only when you choose prayer/reminder notifications.
